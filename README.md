# 🌐 Sarvatra App – On-Demand Services Platform

**Sarvatra** is a service-based Android application that offers a seamless platform to book essential on-demand services—right from your smartphone. Whether it’s beauty care, home repairs, cleaning, or local delivery, Sarvatra connects users with trusted professionals in just a few taps.

## 🔧 Built With

- ☕ **Java & Android SDK** – for native Android development  
- 🎨 **Figma** – for UI/UX wireframing and design  
- 🧩 **XML Layouts** – for responsive and structured UI

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 🧖‍♀️ **Beauty Services** at your doorstep  
- 🔧 **Home Repairs** by verified professionals  
- 🧹 **Cleaning Services** for homes and offices  
- 📦 **Local Delivery Support** across locations  
- 📱 **Minimal & Intuitive UI** designed for mobile-first experience  
- 📍 **Service Booking & Tracking**  

---

## 🎨 UI/UX Design

The entire interface was planned and prototyped in **Figma** to ensure:

- Smooth navigation flow  
- Simple, user-centric layouts  
- Consistent and visually appealing components
